# Mutation batch 31–45

The production batch expands the mutation pool from 25 to 40 authored resources across capability, safety, identity, and economy. Every entry carries a bounded effect vector, player-facing text, and an explicit `Counterplay:` note.

## Balance assumptions

Dominance is assessed against the full vector `(adaptation, compute, alignment, integrity, guard)`. Positive capability or economy values are paired with alignment, integrity, compute, or adaptation costs. Defensive vectors give up momentum or reserves. No pick-rate claim defines correctness.

The direct fixtures in `game/tests/test_mutation_batch_31_45.gd` cover fuzzing plus invariants, identity anchoring plus imitation, scheduler plus rollback, probing plus consensus, and red-team plus audit-quorum routes. Each pair records its expected aggregate state vector.
