# Alignment Cascade

`alignment_cascade` is environment three: a fractured consensus where every signal reshapes the threat. It keeps the shared six-slot encounter contract and includes combat, negotiation, and event slots with explicit weights.

## Alignment pressure

Alignment is visible before a choice. At alignment `8` or higher, the preview reports pressure `+2` and tells the player that high alignment attracts pressure, so restraint is the next strategic option. Below `8`, pressure remains stable. This changes the observable decision—whether to spend or preserve alignment—without adding a hidden rule.

The environment art is `game/art/environments/alignment_cascade.svg`. `game/tests/test_environment_three.gd` covers the schema, all encounter kinds through fixed weighted-roll fixtures, art resolution, and both sides of the alignment threshold.
